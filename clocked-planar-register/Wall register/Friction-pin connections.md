# Friction-pin connections

The mechanism uses LEGO friction pins, without screws, bolts or nuts.

- Fixed bearing walls and removable frame fixtures have at least two spaced mounting pins. Broad controller fixtures have four.
- Each of the nine removable rod-guide caps has two LEGO 2780 pins.
- The detachable WRITE bearing shoe uses two 2780 pins to connect to the moving rod.
- Front and rear actuator bearing cheeks share two LEGO 6558 **3L** friction pins per actuator. Seat the long ends through the rear cheek into the carrier, then press the front cheek onto the exposed short ends.
- Each CLOCK/WRITE rod splice has a bridge and two 2780 pins. Pins carry axial load in shear. Seat pins in the rod shoes first, then press on the bridge.
- The two horizontal joining pins between the printable bit-frame halves are retained.

Install pins from mating faces; do not force their centre collars through nominal 5 mm holes. The revised 2780 sockets include 6.6 mm collar reliefs covering the complete collar length. Actual friction fit and durability require a small print trial.

See [assembly order and print orientations](Assembly%20and%20print%20revision.md). The JSON connection schedules identify each pin and its mating parts; the model inventory lists quantities. This is a development assembly, not a measured strength qualification.
