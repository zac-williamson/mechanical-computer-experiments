A smaller, accessible arrangement

P and C share one A-driven shaft, removing the old A distribution train. B′ drives P’s selector through a coaxial connection. Separate routes distribute P and carry-in, with gear parity compensating for the two reversed actuators.

No stage sits above another. The sum stage’s shaft line is raised 16 mm on its own supports to clear the carry selector. The base area is 8.9% smaller than the old 200 × 172 mm board; external input axle ends project beyond it. The full hardware envelope is 192.6 × 182 mm—about 1.5% larger in plan than the old adder. Overall footprint reduction remains unfinished.

For subtraction, set SU = 1 and feed SU into the least-significant bit’s Cin. Higher bits receive the preceding Cout. View all rotations from the same positive shaft-axis end: 0 = clockwise, 1 = anticlockwise.
