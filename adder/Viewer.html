How the shafts connect

A shared !B′ shaft feeds both the Cin stage and carry clutch. P drives one actuator, whose pinned carriage tie moves both carry and sum clutches. A is shared by those two clutches. Cin only drives its own worm actuator; no clutch takes drive from Cin.

The carry branch uses !B′ on its input shaft: one gear mesh restores B′ at the clutch. The sum train delivers S directly. Cout and S are separate coaxial axles with a gap between them, not a shared output shaft.

This revision reduces both gear count and footprint. The revised base is 292 × 111 mm, down from 308 × 131 mm. The B′ feed uses five gears rather than seven; the Cin stage is 16 mm lower in Z. Guide axles and bearing spacing are reduced.
