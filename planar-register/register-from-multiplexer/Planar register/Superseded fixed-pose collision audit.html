Required coupled motion

During carriage travel, the reaction gear bears against the constrained lever. The worm continues rotating and the carriage translates. During endpoint overrun, the lever releases the reaction gear, allowing it to rotate while carriage travel stops. Worm angle, reaction-gear angle, lever contact and carriage position must therefore be solved together with direction and backlash history. Independently prescribing them is invalid.

Why the earlier checks missed this

The viewer moves the lever but leaves the reaction gear and worm unrotated. Earlier checks either omitted that pair or tested different, prescribed gear poses. The reference gear phase clears the lever in five diagnostic poses, but worm/reaction-gear material-overlap samples remain. No change has been made merely to hide these failures.

Additional all-part diagnostic

All 95 displayed parts, including LEGO hardware, connector and band, were included in a separate five-pose all-pair material scan. It reports every sampled overlap, including friction-pin fits and shaft interfaces, without blanket group exclusions. Worm/gear, input gear/idler, clutch and bore/axle contacts remain unresolved. The LEGO triangle meshes are open and the connector is only a filled envelope, so those sampled volumes cannot yet be treated as reliable physical penetration measurements. They are not passes.

The all-pair scan is finite (0.4 mm or finer sample spacing); it is not a swept-volume proof. The full-frame check above establishes a concrete failure, not clearance of every other pair. Force-driven motion, backlash, loaded clutch release and 0.1 Nm strength remain unverified.
