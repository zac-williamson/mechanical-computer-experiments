# Direct-cam criteria audit

The direct-cam revision removes the crank and its support scaffolding, integrates the bolt guide into the existing bearing cheek, and lowers the rear backbone to the actual mounting regions. It uses 19 printed parts and 80 modelled LEGO pieces.

Original multiplexer worm/reaction/rocking-lever operation and bearing print faces are retained. Moving and structural additions use genuine LEGO axles, bushes and friction pins. The separate cam plate has a fully supported flat print face.

Current numerical evidence is in Operation checks.json, Operation hardware checks.json, Rotating clearance checks.json, Actuator addition checks.json, Actuator band checks.json, Qualification.json, Carriage printing checks.json and Criteria audit evidence.json. These reports have different scopes; none is a physical qualification.

The principal remaining risks are loaded clutch engagement, the steep cam's sensitivity to guide friction, actual pin/axle fits, local contact stress, print quality, and survival of 0.1 Nm in all blocked conditions. Flex screening is conditional, and inherited native LEGO meshes include open surfaces. Full compliance with the original working/torque/continuous-collision criteria is not established.
